Notation Instruments

A small set of browser-based, single-file audio and notation tools — playable surfaces, generative scores, and analysis utilities. Each one opens as a standalone page; nothing to install.

Scale Field Instrument

A generative visual score played by drawing across it — waveform, scale, and tempo are all live and rendered as a shifting field.

Open

Chord Fold Instrument

Drag to fold a plane into a chord — density, spread, and inversion follow the gesture. Store snapshots, sequence them, export MIDI or a PNG score.

Open

Sound Analyzer

Mel spectrogram, chroma, and onset detection on a loaded audio file, live — export to JSON, CSV, or MIDI, plus a poster snapshot.

Open

Ensemble Performance Controller

Four independent players on one shared clock — cueable groups, transpose, scale-guard, and five generative pattern engines.

Open

Note Generator

Five pattern engines turn a key, scale, and tempo into a note sequence — piano-roll preview, export to CSV or MIDI.
